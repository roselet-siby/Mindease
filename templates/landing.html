What is MindEase?

MindEase is an AI companion trained to notice emotion and sentiment in what you write, then respond to the person behind the words — not just the words themselves. No judgment, no waiting room, no small talk you didn't ask for.

How it works

1
Say what's on your mind
Type freely, like you would to a friend. There's no right way to start.
2
MindEase reads the room
Trained models pick up on your emotion and tone as you write, in real time.
3
You get a reply that fits
Shaped around what you actually said — not a generic, one-size-fits-all line.
4
Carry it forward if you choose
Turn your patterns into a private report to bring to a doctor or counsellor.
⚠️
A support tool, not a replacement for therapy
MindEase can't diagnose conditions or give medical advice. If you're in crisis or having thoughts of self-harm, please reach out to a professional right away.
